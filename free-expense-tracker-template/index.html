What’s inside

What’s inside the sheet

Two tabs, ready to use, with 14 sample rows already filled in (April–July 2026) so you can see it working before you touch it:

  • Expenses — one row per expense: date, vendor, category, amount, notes. Categories cover the basics — Rent, Utilities, Software, Office Supplies, Marketing, Travel, Meals, Professional Services — or rename them to fit your business.
  • Dashboard — the rollup: KPI tiles for Total Spend, Biggest Category, and Monthly Average, a Spend by Category table, and a Monthly Trend table with a line chart, all built on SUMIF/SUMIFS formulas that recalculate the moment you add a row.

The math it does for you:

  • Total spend — every logged expense, added up live.
  • Spend by category — a SUMIF rollup per category, so you can see what’s actually eating your budget.
  • Monthly trend — a SUMIFS table by month, charted, so you can see if spend is climbing or flat.
  • Biggest category & monthly average — calculated for you on the Dashboard tile row, no scrolling required.

Header row is frozen, amount columns are currency-formatted, and the categories already match a typical small-business chart of accounts — open it and it just works.

Questions people ask

Expense tracking in Google Sheets, answered

Is this expense tracker template really free?
Yes. It’s a Google Sheets template — you make your own copy and it’s yours to keep and edit, no cost, no card, no trial clock. The paid product is a separate, self-updating version ($650) that connects to your real books; this manual one is free, permanently.
Do I need to know spreadsheet formulas to use it?
No. You only ever type into the Expenses tab — date, vendor, category, amount, notes. The Dashboard tab’s totals, category breakdown, and trend chart are pre-built with SUMIF/SUMIFS formulas that update themselves; you never touch a formula.
Can I add my own expense categories?
Yes. The sample sheet ships with eight common categories (Rent, Utilities, Software, Office Supplies, Marketing, Travel, Meals, Professional Services), but they’re just text in a column — add, rename, or remove categories freely and the Dashboard’s SUMIF rollups will pick up whatever you type.
How is this different from QuickBooks or other accounting software?
This is a lightweight tracker, not full accounting software — no chart of accounts, reconciliation, tax forms, or invoicing. It’s for a fast, free, at-a-glance view of what you’re spending and where. If you want it actually connected to your bank feed, QuickBooks export, or books, that’s the $650 automated version below.
What if I run more than one business or entity?
This free template is built for one set of books at a time — make a separate copy per entity if you run more than one. If you want a single dashboard that rolls up multiple entities or locations automatically, that’s exactly the kind of custom build the $650 Automated Expense Dashboard handles.
