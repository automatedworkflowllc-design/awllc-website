Money Leak Finder

In plain English

What it does
Lines up the work you finished against the invoices you sent, and shows you only the places they do not match.
Why it helps
It finds money you already earned but never collected — jobs completed and never billed, and invoices that quietly went unpaid. It will even write the follow-up emails for you, though it cannot send them; you always press send.
What you need
Two files you already have: a list of jobs or work done, and a list of invoices.
How long
About ten seconds. Your books never leave your machine.

Two exports every business already has — the work log (what got done) and the invoices (what got billed). This page lines them up and shows only the mismatches: finished jobs nobody ever invoiced, and invoices sitting unpaid.

Your books never leave your machine. The matching runs entirely in your browser — there is no server to send these files to. Check the network tab: zero requests after load.
