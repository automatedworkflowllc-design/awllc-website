Shift Coverage Check

In plain English

What it does
Reads your staff schedule and points out the four problems that cost money: a shift nobody is covering, someone drifting into overtime, a job only one person can do, and shifts too close together to be safe.
Why it helps
These are normally noticed on Friday — after the overtime is already owed or the shift already went uncovered. This finds them while you can still move someone.
What you need
Your schedule export: who works, what role, which day.
How long
About ten seconds. Nothing about your staff is uploaded anywhere.

For anyone who runs on a roster — senior living, clinics, restaurants, facilities, security.
