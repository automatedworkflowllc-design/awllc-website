Duplicate Customer Finder

In plain English

What it does
Finds the same customer entered more than once under slightly different names — Acme Roofing, Acme Roofing LLC, acme roofing inc.
Why it helps
Your customer count is wrong, and one customer's history is split across three records — so your best client may not look like your best client. It also stops the mail merge sending the same person three copies.
What you need
One file with a customer or company name column.
How long
About ten seconds, and it tells you why it grouped each one so you can disagree.

Acme Roofing. Acme Roofing LLC. acme roofing inc. Three rows, one customer — and now your customer count is wrong, their revenue history is split three ways, and the mail merge sends them three copies.

Nothing uploads. The matching runs entirely in your browser — there is no server to send your customer list to. Check the network tab: zero requests after load.
