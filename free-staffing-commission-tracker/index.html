What it is / who it’s for

A commission tracker built for how recruiters actually get paid

Most “commission trackers” are generic sales sheets that don’t know what a placement is. This one does. It handles the two ways staffing money actually comes in — a perm fee (a percent of first-year salary) and a contract margin (the spread between your bill rate and pay rate) — and it tracks the thing that quietly costs recruiters the most: fall-offs inside the guarantee window, where a placement leaves and the fee gets clawed back.

It’s a real Google Sheet. You make a copy, you own it, it’s free.

What’s inside

What’s inside the sheet

Five tabs, ready to use, with sample rows already filled in so you can see it working:

  • Placements — one row per deal: candidate, client, role, perm or contract, salary/fee or bill-pay-hours, owner + sourcer split, status, and guarantee days. Type the deal; the money columns calculate themselves.
  • Recruiters — your people, their monthly draw, and their default split. Shows each person’s earned-this-month and their draw balance (earned minus draw).
  • Settings — your commission rules and all the dropdown lists in one place: default perm fee %, default owner/sourcer split, guarantee days.
  • Dashboard — the rollup: total gross, net after fall-offs, pending vs. paid, per-recruiter earnings, and a live clawback-risk list of every deal still inside its 30/60/90-day guarantee.
  • Read Me — a 4-step start guide so you’re logging deals in two minutes.

The math it does for you:

  • Perm fee — first-year salary × your fee %.
  • Contract margin — (bill rate − pay rate) × hours/week × weeks.
  • Splits — owner % vs. sourcer %, per deal, overridable.
  • Draw reconciliation — commission earned minus the draw you’ve advanced.
  • Clawback / fall-off flag — every placement still inside its guarantee window gets flagged ⚠ AT RISK; mark one “Fell Off = Yes” and its commission reverses automatically.

Questions people ask

Recruiter commission tracking, answered

How do you calculate recruiter commission on a placement?
Two ways, depending on the placement. Perm: the fee is a percent of the candidate’s first-year salary (20% on a $100k hire = a $20,000 fee). Contract: you earn the margin — the spread between your bill rate and the pay rate — times hours per week times weeks. This free spreadsheet does both automatically; you just type the numbers in.
What is a commission clawback / fall-off in staffing?
A fall-off is when a placed candidate leaves (quits or is let go) inside the guarantee window you gave the client — usually 30, 60, or 90 days — and the fee gets reversed or refunded, so the commission is “clawed back.” The tracker flags every placement still inside its window as ⚠ AT RISK, and reverses the commission automatically when you mark a deal as fallen off.

Does it handle splits and recruiter draws?
